Verdict

Proceed with the architecture, but do not freeze the central public types yet.

image4s is further along than the first locus4s implementation. Its representation and storage discipline are already unusually strong: one canonical dense image value, one underlying immutable Ravel array, logical axes separated from physical storage layout, zero-copy views, explicit copy boundaries, and a clean division between image representation and production resampling. Those are cornerstone-quality decisions.

The remaining problems are mostly semantic, not mechanical:

Area	Assessment
Canonical dense representation	Keep
Ravel/image4s boundary	Keep
Logical versus physical layout	Keep
Spatial and non-spatial views	Keep and generalize
Non-spatial sampling semantics	Foundationally insufficient
Frame/grid identity	Needs the same repair as locus4s
Sampling compatibility and equality	Currently unsound
Geometry dimensionality	Major medical-imaging decision still unresolved
Image roles and aliases	Overstate what the types prove
Image algebra	Incomplete
NIfTI	Good correctness-oriented beginning, not yet production-grade
Laws	Strong executable contracts, thin reusable algebraic laws
